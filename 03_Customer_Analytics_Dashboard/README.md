# 🛍️ Customer Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

This project showcases an interactive **Customer Analytics Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Power BI** using retail sales data. The dashboard helps businesses analyze customer behavior, revenue trends, profitability, and purchasing patterns to support data-driven decision-making and customer relationship management.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to understand customer purchasing behavior, identify high-value customers, analyze profitability, and evaluate revenue performance across regions and market segments.

---

## Business Insights

- The business generated **$2.30M** in revenue from **5,009 orders** across **793 unique customers**.
- The **Consumer** segment contributed over **50%** of total revenue, making it the primary revenue source.
- A small group of top customers contributed a significant share of revenue, highlighting opportunities for key account management.
- **Technology** was the highest revenue-generating product category, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.
- Revenue trends reveal seasonal fluctuations that can support forecasting and inventory planning.
- Overall profit margin of **12.47%** indicates healthy business performance.
