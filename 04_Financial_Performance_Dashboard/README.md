# 💰 Financial Performance Dashboard

## Overview

This project showcases an interactive **Financial Performance Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Power BI** to monitor revenue, profitability, costs, discounts, and sales performance across products, countries, and customer segments. The dashboard helps executives evaluate financial health and identify opportunities for business growth.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to provide a comprehensive financial overview by tracking revenue, profit, costs, margins, product performance, and regional contributions to support strategic financial decision-making.

## Business Insights

- The business generated **$118.73M** in revenue with a **14.23%** profit margin.
- The **Government** segment contributed the highest share of total profit.
- **Paseo** was the highest revenue-generating product, followed by VTT and Velo.
- The **High Discount Band** accounted for the largest discount amount, highlighting opportunities to optimize discount strategies.
- The **United States** contributed the highest overall profit, making it the most valuable market.
- Monthly revenue trends indicate seasonal variations that can support forecasting and budget planning.
