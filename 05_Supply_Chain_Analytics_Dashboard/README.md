# 🚚 Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive **Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Power BI** to monitor logistics performance, revenue, profitability, inventory movement, shipping efficiency, and regional sales trends. The dashboard enables supply chain managers to optimize operations through data-driven insights.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to analyze supply chain operations, evaluate shipping performance, monitor delivery efficiency, and identify revenue-generating regions and product categories to improve operational decision-making.

## Business Insights

- Processed **66K customer orders**, generating **$36.78M** in revenue and **$3.97M** in profit.
- Achieved an overall **10.78% profit margin**, reflecting stable operational performance.
- **Western Europe** generated the highest revenue among all regions.
- **Standard Class** accounted for the majority of shipments, making it the primary shipping method.
- Most deliveries were completed successfully with an average delivery time of **3.5 days**.
- Revenue distribution by category highlights **Fishing** as the highest-performing product category.
