# 👥 HR Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built in **Microsoft Power BI** to help organizations monitor workforce trends, employee attrition, demographics, and key HR metrics. The dashboard enables HR professionals to identify retention challenges and make data-driven workforce decisions.

---

## Business Objective

The goal of this dashboard is to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary distribution, and departmental performance to improve employee retention and strategic HR planning.

## Business Insights

- Overall employee attrition rate stands at **16.12%**.
- Research & Development experienced the highest employee attrition.
- Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives recorded the largest number of employees leaving.
- Most employee attrition occurred in the **26–35** age group.
- Employees in the **Low Salary Band** showed the highest attrition.
- Workforce composition is approximately **60% Male** and **40% Female**.
